Python Skin Listing Monitor

A browser port of the app.R Shiny app in this repo — same keyword lists and scoring rule, runs entirely client-side. Built for the BL5314 "Show Me the Source" group project.

A flag is not proof that a listing is illegal. This tool only sorts listings for a human reviewer, who makes the final call. Method follows Di Minin et al. (2019) and Xu et al. (2019). Sample listings are fictional, for demonstration only.

Columns: id, platform, date, account, text, price. Without a file, the 14 fictional sample listings are used.

Changes apply as soon as you click out of a field. Add new slang or local-language terms here.
